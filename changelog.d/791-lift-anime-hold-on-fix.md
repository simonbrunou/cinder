### Fixed
- **An anime hold lifts as soon as you make its fix (#791).** A movie or series held on its
  Anime preferences ("Needs preferences") used to keep its hold, and keep counting in the
  Activity badge, until a sweep visited it again, which for some series never happened. Now
  changing the title's profile or Audio pick, saving the subtitle languages or anime preferences
  in Settings, or unmonitoring a series' last monitored episode lifts the hold at once, unless the
  preferences still fail. An Auto-profile Japanese animation with preferences that cannot be met
  also keeps a steady hold instead of losing and regaining it on every sweep.
