### Fixed
- **A finished download waits for its fake-file check when its download folder does not answer (#946).** The check that looks for a fake on disk (with the default blocked and media lists, a shortcut, an installer or a script with no video, no archive and no book in a format Cinder imports beside it), on by default, reads the download folder once the client reports the download finished. When that read timed out on a stuck mount, Cinder recorded the download as finished without the check. It now keeps the download in progress, logs a warning, and checks the folder again on each poll until the read answers. A fake found then is handled like any other blocked download. Nothing limits the wait, as for the import that follows. While it waits, a download client that drops the finished job counts against the download's retry budget.
