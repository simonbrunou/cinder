### Security
- **`phoenix` 1.8.9 → 1.8.17 (CVE-2026-129344, CVE-2026-128361).** Two HIGH advisories against
  Phoenix's 1.8 line up to, but not including, 1.8.17. In the first, a client that joins a channel
  topic with the legacy V1 serializer can make a `{:binary, data}` broadcast on that topic crash
  the process that dispatches it. In the second, a map used as a `Phoenix.Presence` key, such as
  a JSON object from a join payload, or a `fetch/2` that raises on a tracked key, can crash the
  Presence tracker. 1.8.17 needs `phoenix_pubsub` 2.4, so `phoenix_pubsub` moves from 2.2.0 to
  2.4.1 too. After osv.dev published both advisories on 2026-11-21, `mix hex.audit`, which CI's
  required `dependency-audit` job runs, failed on a `mix.lock` that held 1.8.9.
