### Changed
- **A standard series' "Find a better match" panel marks a release that covers no wanted episode
  (#900).** A row whose release covers no episode the season still wants, such as a season pack
  in a season you already have or a single of an episode already imported, now says "covers no
  wanted episode". The season's automatic search never takes such a release for its wanted
  episodes. The note changes neither the row's verdict, its place, nor whether you
  can grab it as an upgrade. Anime panels are unchanged.
