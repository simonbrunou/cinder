### Security
- **`bandit` 1.13.1 → 1.14.0 (CVE-2026-108525 and ten more).** Eleven advisories against Bandit
  1.13.1, nine HIGH. Most let a client tie up the server's memory or CPU: unbounded HTTP/2 DATA
  and header state, quadratic work on HTTP/2 control frames and on `Content-Length` and port
  parsing, 1-byte HTTP/1.1 chunks and WebSocket continuation frames. Chunk extensions with a bare
  CR or LF also allowed request smuggling, and after the server sent a WebSocket close, Bandit
  kept sending frames and passing the peer's frames to the handler. 1.14.0 fixes all eleven. It
  also caps concurrent HTTP/2 streams at 500 and answers a malformed HTTP/1 request line with
  400. The advisories also failed the required `mix hex.audit` CI check on every PR.
