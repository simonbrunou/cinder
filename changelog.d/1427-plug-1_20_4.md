### Security
- **`plug` 1.20.3 → 1.20.4 (CVE-2026-120287).** A MEDIUM advisory against Plug: its multipart
  parser did not charge part and header framing, such as empty headers, against the body's
  `:length` limit. Cinder's endpoint parses multipart bodies before its router runs, so before
  any sign-in check. 1.20.4 counts that framing. The advisory also failed the required
  `mix hex.audit` CI check on every PR.
