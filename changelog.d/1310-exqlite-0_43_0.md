### Fixed
- **A wait for the database write lock no longer freezes the whole app (#1310).** exqlite, the
  SQLite driver, moves from 0.37.0 to 0.43.0. With 0.37.0, while one database connection waited
  for another's write lock, a process that freed a prepared statement of the waiting connection
  blocked one of the Erlang VM's schedulers until the wait ended, after up to the 5 s busy
  timeout. Other processes stalled with it, and when the lock holder was among them it could not
  commit in time: the waiter then failed with `database is locked`, and a refresher or poller
  skipped that series, book work or movie until its next pass.
  With 0.43.0, freeing such a statement no longer waits for its connection, so a wait for the
  lock holds back only its own caller. A transaction that holds the lock for more than 5 s still
  fails a waiter with `database is locked`. The bundled SQLite moves from 3.53.2 to 3.54.1.
